# Forms

To make forms as straightforward as possible, there's a `<Form />` wrapper that works with a
 [Yup](https://github.com/jquense/yup) schema to give you all your frontend validation, layout,
 labels and the like from the start.

 The easiest way to understand how it works is to look at the stories for the individual component
 types.
